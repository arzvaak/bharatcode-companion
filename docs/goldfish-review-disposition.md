## New owner decisions after review inputs

The [current brief](../concepts_brief.md#L25-L35) replaces the former whole-building supply gate. Construction may begin with partial supplies and complete safe independent steps. The companion must hold materials before it uses them. It may acquire shortages using already-authorized gathering/crafting within approved project budgets and current permissions, asking before exceeding them. It must still obey dependency, approval, removal, protection, and real-accounting rules.

Consequently, architecture F3/F10, maintenance M-04, migration MIG-11, inventive IC-10, and any other complete-material start requirement describe superseded scope. Their accounting, dependency, manual-placement and truthful-progress concerns remain useful. Logical reservations are not physical stock and cannot be consumed as such, even though collecting the entire bill upfront is no longer required. The earlier choice to hold the complete bill in companion storage is also superseded; custody-before-use remains.

This changed incremental policy has not received a fresh independent review. No reviewer is represented as having approved a decision made after its inputs were fixed.

## Remaining design choices and evidence limits

Define approval/delegation and expected versus unexpected site changes; natural-source evidence in existing worlds; storage exhaustion and ambiguous external transfers; manual handoff/verification and waiting; offline reservation fairness; safe recall; ancillary supplies; external machine continuation; experiment effects and commissioning evidence; AI spending and privacy-safe retention; and upgrade/uninstall boundaries. These are design questions, not proof of runtime defects.

Journals, locks, single-writer services, capability tuples, caches, caps, tracing, metering and schema fields remain candidate mechanisms. No mechanism is adopted merely because a reviewer recommended it. Permission caching must preserve checks before every edit/interaction. Technical backpressure must not introduce an undisclosed gameplay carrying cap.

No runtime tests were executed. Existing scenarios remain unexecuted, and no exact ATM10 integration, provider route, API, performance figure, persistence guarantee or protection configuration has been verified.
